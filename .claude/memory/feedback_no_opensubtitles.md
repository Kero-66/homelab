Never suggest OpenSubtitles for subtitle sourcing.

**Why:** No longer free or personally usable for self-hosted setups.

**How to apply:** When discussing subtitle sources, stick to alternatives like Kitsunekko, AnimeTosho, subliminal (with other providers), or manual fansub sourcing.
